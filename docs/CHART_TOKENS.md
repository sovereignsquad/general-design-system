# Chart Tokens

Status: Active SSOT
Version: 6.8.0
Last updated: 2026-11-13

The `--gds-chart-*` roles colour the GDS chart renderers (`GdsAreaChart`, `GdsLineChart` and the
other wrappers in `packages/gds-core/src/SemanticCharts.tsx`) and the `ChartTokenPanel` legend.
Source: `packages/gds-theme/src/chart-tokens.ts`.

## Roles

| Role | Value | Contract |
|---|---|---|
| `--gds-chart-series-1` | The lane's `--gds-brand-accent` (falls back to `--gds-brand-primary`) | `GDS_CHART_SERIES_MIN_CONTRAST` against `--gds-bg-card` |
| `--gds-chart-series-2` to `-8` | The default accent axis bases (`GDS_DEFAULT_ACCENT_AXIS`), in candidate order, skipping hues too close to an earlier series | Same; the eight values are distinct |
| `--gds-chart-axis-text` | The lane's `--gds-text-meta` | `GDS_CHART_AXIS_TEXT_MIN_CONTRAST` against `--gds-bg-card` |
| `--gds-chart-grid` | The lane's `--gds-border-card` | Decorative; not contrast-checked |
| `--gds-chart-area-opacity-start` / `-end` | `GDS_CHART_AREA_OPACITY` | Scheme-independent; declared once in `styles.css` |

Each colour role is emitted per preset and scheme by `emitChartCssVariables()` with the other
semantic roles (`-dark` suffix for the dark scheme), so the vibe presets, the Class USA and Gold
Athlete lanes and `createBrandTheme(...)` all carry them. For `class-usa` and `gold-athlete` the
roles are recomputed after the hand-authored layer, so series 1 follows their own accent. A
colour that falls short of its contract is mixed toward black (light scheme) or white (dark
scheme) until it passes; in the dark scheme the accent bases are first lifted toward white so
they keep their hue.

Lanes without the preset runtime (`gdsTheme`, `gdsDarkPublicTheme`, `createPublicBrandTheme`)
read the `gds:chart-defaults` block in `packages/gds-theme/styles.css`: the `default` preset's
values as `light-dark()` pairs plus the two opacity stops, generated by `npm run tokens:axis-css`.

## Renderers

- **Area**: a vertical `linearGradient` from `--gds-chart-area-opacity-start` at the top to
  `-end` at the baseline, in the series colour. Each chart gets its own gradient id.
- **Line and area**: four horizontal gridlines in `--gds-chart-grid`; the plot fills the width
  of its container and strokes keep their width when it scales.
- **Axis labels**: `axisLabels` on the line and area wrappers labels the first, middle and last
  data points under the plot in `--gds-chart-axis-text`. Off by default.
- **Series colour**: single-series wrappers still take `seriesTone` (`gdsSeriesPalette`).
  `getGdsChartSeriesColor(n)` returns `var(--gds-chart-series-n)` for categorical series;
  indexes past 8 wrap to slot 1. `GDS_CHART_SERIES_COLORS` lists the eight slots in order.

## Legend

`ChartTokenPanel` renders each legend entry as a colour swatch and its label in a list labelled
"Chart legend". The `token` is resolved by `resolveGdsChartTokenColor()` and painted as the
swatch; it is not rendered as text. Accepted forms: a series tone (`primary`), a custom
property (`--gds-chart-series-2`), a dotted role id (`chart.series-2` reads
`--gds-chart-series-2`), or a CSS colour value. The `GdsChart` default legend uses
`chart.series-1` and `chart.series-2`.

## Forced colours

Under `forced-colors: active` (rules at the end of `styles.css`): series lines take
`CanvasText`, the area gradient is not painted, gridlines take `GrayText`, axis labels take
`CanvasText`, and each legend swatch keeps its colour inside a `CanvasText` outline. The legend
labels and the accessible data table carry the meaning.

## Gates

- `npm run verify:a11y-floor`, rule `chart-roles-legible`: every series and the axis text meet
  their contract on the card, the roles are emitted, and the eight series are distinct, for
  every preset in both schemes.
- `npm run verify:axis-css`: the `gds:chart-defaults` block matches its source.
- `npm run verify:default-lane-axis-tokens`: every `--gds-chart-*` read without a fallback
  resolves in the non-preset lanes.
- `npm run verify:forced-colors-runtime`: on `/patterns/data`, for each preset in its widened case list, the series
  line computes to the text colour, the area fill to `none`, gridlines have a stroke, and the
  legend swatch has a solid outline.
- Tests: `packages/gds-theme/src/chart-tokens.test.ts`, `packages/gds-core/src/chart-tokens.test.tsx`.

Not covered yet: multi-series rendering and a toggleable legend (#915).
